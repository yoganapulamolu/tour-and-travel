Varanasi

Varanasi is one of the oldest living cities in the world, located on the banks of the River Ganga in Uttar Pradesh.

It is famous for Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat and spiritual atmosphere.

Best Time to Visit: October to March
