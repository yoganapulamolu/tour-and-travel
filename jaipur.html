About Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital city of Rajasthan and is known as the "Pink City" because of its beautiful pink-colored buildings. It was founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II.

Jaipur is famous for its royal palaces, historic forts, traditional markets, and rich cultural heritage. It is one of the most popular tourist destinations in India.

Famous Attractions

  • Hawa Mahal
  • Amber Fort
  • City Palace
  • Jantar Mantar
  • Jal Mahal
